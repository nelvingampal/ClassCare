TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Effective Date: September 2026
Application Name: ClassCare

Welcome to ClassCare, a student attendance and tardiness monitoring application designed to help schools, teachers, students, and parents efficiently monitor class attendance. The application uses QR codes and QR scanning technology to record attendance and tardiness and may send email notifications to registered parents or guardians.

By accessing or using the application, you agree to comply with these Terms and Conditions.

1. Purpose of the Application

The application is developed to:

  • Record student attendance through QR code scanning;
  • Monitor and record student tardiness;
  • Maintain organized digital attendance records;
  • Provide authorized teachers and school personnel with attendance information; and
  • Notify registered parents or guardians when their child records attendance.

The application is intended solely for educational and attendance-monitoring purposes.

2. QR Code Attendance and Tardiness

Each student may be assigned a unique QR code that is used to record attendance.
When a student's QR code is scanned, the system may record the following:

  • Student name;
  • Class or subject;
  • Date of attendance;
  • Time of scanning; and
  • Attendance status, such as Present, Late, or Absent, based on the school's attendance rules.

Students must use only their own assigned QR code.
Students are prohibited from:

  • Using another student's QR code;
  • Allowing another person to scan their QR code;
  • Sharing their QR code for attendance purposes;
  • Creating or modifying QR codes;
  • Attempting to manipulate attendance or tardiness records; or
  • Circumventing the QR scanning system.

3. Attendance Records

Attendance and tardiness records generated through the application are intended to assist teachers and school administrators in monitoring student attendance.

The application records attendance based on information captured through the QR scanning system. However, technical problems such as scanning errors, device malfunction, internet connection problems, or system interruptions may affect the recording of attendance.

If a student believes that their attendance or tardiness record is incorrect, they should immediately report the concern to the appropriate teacher or school administrator for verification.

4. Parent/Guardian Email Notifications

The application may automatically send an email notification to the registered parent or legal guardian when their child successfully records attendance.
The notification may contain:

  • Student's name;
  • Class or subject;
  • Date;
  • Time of attendance; and
  • Attendance status.

These notifications are provided to help parents or guardians monitor their child's attendance and are intended for legitimate educational and monitoring purposes.

Parents or guardians are responsible for providing and maintaining a valid email address. The application may not be able to deliver notifications if the email address is incorrect, inactive, inaccessible, or if technical or internet-related problems occur.

5. Information Collected

To provide its attendance-monitoring services, the application may collect and process information such as:

  • Student name;
  • Student identification number;
  • Class, section, or program;
  • QR code or QR code identifier;
  • Parent or guardian name;
  • Parent or guardian email address;
  • Attendance date and time;
  • Attendance and tardiness status; and
  • Other information necessary for the operation of the application.

The application should only collect information that is necessary and relevant to its intended purpose.

6. Privacy and Protection of Information

The application shall take reasonable measures to protect student, parent, and attendance information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Information collected through the application shall be accessed only by authorized individuals for legitimate educational, administrative, or attendance-monitoring purposes.

Where applicable, the collection, processing, storage, and sharing of personal information shall comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the applicable policies of the educational institution.

7. Use of Information

Attendance information may be used for purposes including:

  • Monitoring student attendance;
  • Identifying patterns of tardiness;
  • Preparing attendance reports;
  • Communicating attendance information to registered parents or guardians;
  • Supporting school attendance management; and
  • Performing other legitimate educational and administrative functions.

The information shall not be used for purposes unrelated to the application's intended function without appropriate authorization.

8. User Responsibilities

Users agree to:

  • Provide accurate information;
  • Use the application only for its intended purpose;
  • Keep their account information and QR code secure;
  • Not share their QR code or login credentials with unauthorized individuals;
  • Report incorrect attendance records to the appropriate school personnel;
  • Respect the privacy of other students; and
  • Follow applicable school rules and policies.

9. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

  • Falsify attendance information;
  • Scan another student's QR code;
  • Share or lend QR codes;
  • Attempt to access another person's account without authorization;
  • Modify attendance records without permission;
  • Attempt to bypass or interfere with the QR scanning system;
  • Introduce malicious software or code into the application; or
  • Use the application for unlawful or unauthorized purposes.

10. System Availability and Technical Issues

The application is designed to provide reliable attendance monitoring. However, continuous availability cannot be guaranteed.
Temporary interruptions may occur due to:

  • Internet connectivity problems;
  • Device or QR scanner malfunction;
  • Server or system maintenance;
  • Software updates;
  • Technical errors; or
  • Other circumstances beyond the reasonable control of the developers or school.

In cases of system failure, the school may use an alternative attendance-recording procedure.

11. Accuracy of Parent Notifications

Email notifications are generated based on attendance information recorded by the application. A notification indicating that a student has attended class does not, by itself, replace the school's official attendance record.

Parents or guardians who believe that an attendance notification is incorrect should contact the appropriate teacher or school administrator.

12. Account and Access

Access to certain features of the application may be limited to authorized students, teachers, parents, guardians, and school administrators.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and must immediately report suspected unauthorized access to the application administrator.

13. Modification of the Application

The developers or authorized school administrators may modify, update, suspend, or discontinue certain features of the application when necessary for maintenance, security, improvement, or compliance with institutional policies.

Users may be notified of significant changes when appropriate.

14. Suspension or Termination of Access

Access to the application may be suspended or terminated if a user:

  • Violates these Terms and Conditions;
  • Misuses the QR attendance system;
  • Attempts to manipulate attendance records;
  • Gains or attempts to gain unauthorized access; or
  • Violates applicable school rules or policies.

15. Limitation of Responsibility

The application serves as a digital attendance and tardiness monitoring tool. While reasonable measures are taken to maintain accurate records, the developers do not guarantee that the system will always be free from technical errors, interruptions, or inaccuracies.

Official decisions regarding attendance, tardiness, and related academic matters remain subject to the policies and verification procedures of the educational institution.

16. Contact and Support

For questions, concerns, attendance corrections, privacy concerns, or technical assistance, users may contact:

  • Application Administrator: School IT Administration Office
  • Email: support@classcare.edu
  • Contact Desk: IT Administration Helpdesk

17. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using ClassCare, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms and Conditions.
